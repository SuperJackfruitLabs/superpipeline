Schedules

Cards that open themselves on a cadence.

No recurring cards on this board yet. Each one you add lists its cadence, time zone, target stage, next and last run, and who set it up, with Pause and Remove.

How to write a cadence

every 30 minutes · every 2 hours · every 3 days · daily at 09:00 · weekly on mon at 09:00 · monthly on 1 at 09:00 (day 1–28). Schedules are checked every five minutes, so a card may open up to five minutes late.
